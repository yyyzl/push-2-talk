# Approved concept

`05a-approved.png` is the user-selected image from the built-in image generation
tool. The final production geometry is reconstructed in `../mark.svg` from this
image, rather than generated again. Later button / press-inset alternatives were
rejected. App and tray resources are deterministic renders of that shared vector.

The concept was generated by refining the preceding 05 / CLICK + RELEASE image
with this prompt (the input image was a design reference, not a competitor logo):

> Use case: logo-brand refinement.
> The attached image is the EXISTING CONCEPT TO REFINE, not a competitor reference.
> Keep its distinctive overall character, confident flat silhouette and
> black-and-white treatment. The product is press-to-speak voice dictation. The
> user likes the abstract direction but wants a subtle, integrated sound-wave or
> microphone cue. Do NOT introduce P/T/any letter or initials.
>
> Produce ONE refined concept, not a page of unrelated alternatives. The visual
> idea must come from the specified edit, not from texture or rendering. Simple
> custom geometry, thick solid shapes, optically generous gaps suitable for a tiny
> menu-bar glyph. Flat pure black and white only. No 3D, gradients, shadows,
> keycaps, badges, sparkle, text inside the mark, or unrelated decoration.
>
> Presentation: square white sheet, small understated sans-serif concept label at
> upper left. A large standalone black symbol in the center. Below, show the SAME
> symbol smaller in a black rounded-square app tile reversed white, and as
> standalone black and white glyphs on light and charcoal horizontal strips. Keep
> these forms consistent. Do not label them with fake pixel dimensions. No product
> wordmark, slogans or prose.
>
> Label: "05A / EMIT".
> Keep the reference's compact low-left source shape, rising upper-right sweep,
> diagonal forward motion, and carefully fitted curved white gap. Integrate a
> SOUND WAVE into the tall right-hand sweep by dividing it with ONE broad
> contour-following curved negative-space channel, producing two bold nested
> sound lobes that fan out upward-right from the left source. Three solid black
> components total, integrated into the existing outline, no thin external ripples.
> Retain the original rising silhouette and rounded corners. Make the left source
> slightly more capsule-like and expressive but not a literal microphone. A clear
> feeling of a small press releasing voice, with a distinctive diagonal acoustic
> geometry, not wifi or a stock speaker icon.
